NeoCast IPTV Player – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 3, 2025

1. Personal Data Collection

NeoCast IPTV Player does not collect or store any personal data from users.

2. Internet and Network Access

The App requires an active internet connection to stream IPTV content from user-added playlist URLs. We do not host or provide any media content ourselves.

3. Biometric Authentication

NeoCast uses biometric authentication (e.g., fingerprint) solely to secure access to user playlists. Biometric data is never stored or transmitted — it is processed locally on your device using the system's built-in biometric API.

4. No Monetization

This app does not include ads, in-app purchases, or any monetization features.

5. Data Sharing

NeoCast IPTV Player does not share any data with third parties.
